01 · Source language

The canvas is the source language.

Color, stroke, and the tension of light and shadow are not mood. They are grammar — autonomous works that stand alone in the gallery.

02 · Continuation

Fashion does not decorate that grammar. It continues it onto the body as limited, conceptual direction — presence worn, never retail.

Where art meets design is not a print on a tee. It is the original, carried in a person’s movement.

The House Language

One house.
Two crafts.
Equal weight.

Like the zebra, ZEHARIA holds a shared language of stripe and form — yet every line is singular. The paintings do not advertise the clothes. The clothes do not diminish the paintings.

Together they tell a story obvious in five seconds: the canvas is the source; the garment is the continuation. Wear the original — not a print of convenience, but the language of the work, carried.
